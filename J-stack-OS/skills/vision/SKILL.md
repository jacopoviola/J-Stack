# Vision Gate
1. Read state, charter, existing constitution, and supplied materials.
2. Retrieve second-brain evidence only when it can materially affect the current decision.
3. Interrogate one question at a time. Challenge assumptions; do not seek agreement.
4. Maintain vision, principles, brand/design rules, non-negotiables, anti-goals, and MLP definition.
5. Distinguish known, inferred, and undecided.
6. When ready, summarize in ≤12 bullets and ask one final question: lock VISION or keep editing?
7. Only explicit human approval can PASS. Stop after the answer.
